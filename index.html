How to participate
CenteVisa

CenteAgent

Visa Debit Card

The CenteVisa Debit Card is a globally accepted card that enables Centenary Bank customers to transact conveniently, at all Visa acceptance points. Access points for the Visa Debit Card include;

  • Online payment platforms e.g. (jumia, amazon and bazebo)
  • Points of Sale machines.
  • Automated Teller Machines (ATM’s)
  • wherever Visa services have been enabled.
    • With this card, you have easy access to your money anytime, anywhere in the world.
